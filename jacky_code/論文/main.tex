\title{EPFD-Aware Beam Reassociation for Service\\Recovery in Multi-Beam LEO Satellite Systems}

\author{Chia-Yu~Hu and Guey-Yun~Chang}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Non-geostationary satellite orbit (NGSO) systems may cause severe interference to geostationary satellite orbit (GSO) systems during near-inline events. Shutting down high-interference beams can satisfy the equivalent power flux-density (EPFD) constraint, but it also interrupts the service of users covered by those beams. This thesis proposes EPFD-Aware Beam Reassociation for Service Recovery (EABR) for multi-beam low Earth orbit satellite systems under NGSO--GSO coexistence. When some beams are shut down to satisfy the EPFD constraint, EABR continues serving the affected users while maintaining EPFD compliance. Simulation results show that EABR improves the quality and continuity of user service. Its average computation time remains below 1\,s per time slot. Overall, EABR provides a practical way to recover user service while satisfying the EPFD constraint.
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
NGSO--GSO coexistence, EPFD, LEO satellite, beam reassociation, service recovery.
\end{IEEEkeywords}
